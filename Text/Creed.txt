We live in a world divided. Not even brothers or sisters, parents and child have commonality,
nor trust in eachother. Our neighborhoods are mere facades of common humanity, our homes are
prisons of loneliness. Social media, and the illusion of choice it gives, fills the voids of
human connection in our souls with hatred, fear, and endless distractions from the oppression
it brings. Social media apps have led to bifurcation of the human condition, between those with
a strong desire for human connection, and those who revile it. Our coworkers are not
acquaintances, our friends are not seen in person, our neighbors, even if they live next door,
are not known. Our youth have dysphoric nostalgia, dystalgia; for a past they never lived,
with feelings they can't put into words. They long for a time when human connection wasn't
conditional, and with caveats. The problems may be complex, but the solution is simple.
Our creed, is that our sole purpose is to turn coworkers from disperate burdens, into edifying
people with common goals. Turn friends from disconnected interactions, into soul building hope
for the future. Turn neighbors from siloed strangers, into friends. If we fail in this goal,
then there is no reason for us to be here, and for as long as I am CEO, I will shut the doors
of this company for good.
