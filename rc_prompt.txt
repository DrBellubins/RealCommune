Description:
I am creating a social media platform that primarily functions with maps
and geolocation.

The basic functionality is the users can't friend people over the internet,
they have to be within the interaction zone (for now is 25 miles). Once
friended they can't chat at any distance.

Also, other users who are not in your friends list that you end up being
around often, and for extended periods of time, show up as grey dots,
with no name (for anonymity). The user can optionally reach out to the
other, and can optionally choose to share their name or not.

There are two sections:
Map - The main map where user dots, and events show up.
Contacts - The page where even if contacts aren't on the map (outside
of the radius), they can still be direct messaged.

The social media platform doesn't have a static "friends" system,
instead it has a loose contacts of people you know, which you the user
can optionally group into custom groups (example: Work friends,
acquaintances, basketball bros, family, close family, etc). These groups,
are not told to other users, and are just a way to keep contacts organized.

There will be a block system, by which User1 can block User2,
which will notify User1 whenever User2 is within the interaction radius.
User2 is never notified that they've been blocked, that is up to User1 to
disclose.

There will be a Do Not Disturb mode, which will prevent other users from
being notified when User1 enters their interaction radius.

The app will primarily be phone-based, in order to have proper geolocation
services. Geolocation will be grabbe infrequently in the background while
the app isn't running.

Details:
Contact icons and colors can be chosen by User1, with the exeption of
green (shown on the map).

Event icons are always green and are based on the type of
event (concert, fair, club, sports game, etc).

People you've been around often and for long periods of
time (neighbors, coworkers, etc) show up as gray dots.

The friend interaction radius will be 25 miles.
The neighbor interaction radius will be 1 mile.

Users outside of the interaction radius don't show up on the map.

The interaction radius between contacts can combine so that if they
are < 50 miles apart, they still show up on the map, but don't notify
User1 till they are within 25 miles of eachother.

Users are only notified of other user's presence if they're not
driving/flying (needs automatic detection, no reliance on airplane mode).

Events:
Events can only be created by special "Event Organizer" accounts, which
can only be added by Commune, the company.
