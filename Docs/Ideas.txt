~ MAPS ~
Other people are shown as dots on the map, clicking on them can open direct messages (only if friends).

Friends are shown as blue dots.
Events are shown as green dots.
People you've been around often & for long periods of time (neighbors, coworkers, etc) show up as gray dots.

~ INTERACTION ~
The friend interaction radius will be 25 miles.
The neighbor interaction radius will be 1 mile.

The interaction radius between friends can combine so that if friends are < 50 miles apart, they still show
up on the map.

If you're within the radius of interaction, friends only are notified of eachothers presence if they're not
driving/flying.

Speech bubbles should be the same color, as to not place importance on either the user's messages, or
their friends's messages.

~ FRIENDS SECTION ~
Friends section only works within 20 meters (because of gps limitations).
Friend requests only show first names, without profile pictures (to mitigate stalking).

~ TECHNICAL ~
We can't rely on gps alone, accelerometer has to be taken into account to detect when a user is driving vs
walking in a building.

~ FINANCIAL ~
Primarily donation based.
Can be used as a donation platform like patreon for local buisnesses/events/outreaches can open donations,
with Commune taking a cut. Each buisness/event/outreach on the map's overlay can have a little donation
button in the top right.
Democratic voting based on total donations of users. (no donations can't vote) to decide platform features.
